Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to own a Rolls-Royce or Bentley?

No. The RREC is an enthusiasts’ club and welcomes people with a genuine interest in the marques.

What does membership include?

Benefits include publications, events, member resources, technical seminars, archives and access to the wider Club community.

How do I join?

Choose the membership option that suits you and complete registration through the Club’s membership service.
