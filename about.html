THE RREC

The Club

For anyone with a genuine interest in Rolls-Royce and Bentley motor cars.

Enthusiasm comes first.

The Rolls-Royce Enthusiasts’ Club is an international community built around a shared appreciation of the cars, people, engineering and heritage of Rolls-Royce and Bentley.

At its heart are local Sections, Model Registers, events, publications and technical resources that connect enthusiasts across generations and borders.
