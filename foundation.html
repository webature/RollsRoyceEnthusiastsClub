Sir Henry Royce
Memorial Foundation

Cherishing the legacy. Promoting the ideals.

Engineering excellence, preserved.

Established in 1977, the Foundation safeguards archival material, records, photographs, artefacts and memorabilia connected with Sir Henry Royce and the motor cars.

Its work also supports education, research, craftsmanship and the pursuit of engineering excellence for future generations.
